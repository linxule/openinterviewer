# Translation review, October 2026

The participant screens and the consent data notice (`src/lib/i18n/messages/`) were drafted in Simplified Chinese, French, Japanese, Korean and Spanish with AI assistance (Claude) on 2026-10-08, then reviewed independently by three other models through cc-fleet: Gemini 3.8 Flash, Kimi K3 and Grok 4.6. Each reviewer received the English source, all five translations and the same instructions: report only real problems, and treat the data notice as close to a legal text that may not add, remove, weaken or strengthen any promise.

These are AI-reviewed translations, not certified ones. A native speaker's review of the data notice is still advisable before a study relies on it; researchers write and own their study's consent text in every language.

## Voice input strings (second round, 2026-10-08)

The voice input strings (`consent.voice`, `interview.voice`) were reviewed the same way. Kimi K3 reported no problems. The Gemini run failed and was not repeated. Grok 4.6 reported:

| Language | Key | Severity | Finding | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| fr | `consent.voice.installation` | major | "n’est pas conservé" reads as retention, not storage | Accepted: "n’est pas stocké". |
| ja | `consent.voice.browser` | major | 処理します adds a processing claim | Rejected: the browser vendor's service does process the speech, which is what the notice discloses. |
| ko | `consent.voice.browser` | major | 처리합니다 adds a processing claim | Rejected, as for Japanese. |
| zh | `consent.voice.browser` | minor | 自带的 (built-in) can suggest on-device processing | Accepted: removed. |

Before release, the installation notice was also corrected in every language after a code review: it no longer says Cloudflare "hosts this study", which is false on the Node target. Its storage sentence was also narrowed from "the recording is not stored" to "this study does not keep the recording": Cloudflare's published Workers AI terms rule out training on it but state no retention period. The fr wording accepted above was superseded by this change.

## Process for later changes

Change `en.ts` first. Any change to `consent.transport`, `consent.commitment` or `consent.controller` needs every translation updated and reviewed again, and a new entry in this folder. `tests/unit/interviewLanguages.test.ts` checks that every language has every message, with no empty or placeholder text, and that each notice names the provider, model and Cloudflare exactly as given.
