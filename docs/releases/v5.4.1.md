# OpenInterviewer 5.4.1

Chinese and Japanese dashes and ellipses now display correctly. Before this fix,
"——" in a Chinese sentence or button, such as 我同意——开始访谈, showed as two
short, separated dashes, and "……" showed as Latin dots.

## What changed

- On Chinese and Japanese participant pages, the dash (U+2014, U+2015) and the
  ellipsis (U+2026) are drawn from a CJK font already installed on the device,
  such as PingFang, Hiragino, Microsoft YaHei or Noto Sans CJK. All other
  characters keep the site's fonts, and nothing new is downloaded.
- The fix covers fixed screens, the researcher's consent text and the AI
  interviewer's replies.
- In the researcher's transcript view, each turn is marked with the interview
  language. The same dashes display correctly there, and screen readers read the
  turn in the right language.
- English and the other interface languages are unchanged.

## Upgrade and rollback

A code-only release, with no schema, configuration or binding changes. Rolling
back to 5.4.0 is safe.
