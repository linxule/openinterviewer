# OpenInterviewer 4.2.0

Researchers now choose what participants are told about the AI provider. This
release also closes the open gaps from the Cloudflare migration: researcher AI
budgets, sign-in limits for IPv6 and on Node, the sample workspace on the AI
Gateway, and removing a provider key.

## What participants are told about the AI provider

Each study now sets **AI Provider → What participants are told**
(`aiProviderCommitment`):

- **Only this provider and model** (the default for new studies). The
  consent data notice adds: *The interview and any later analysis of your
  responses use {Model} ({Provider}); the study does not switch them to
  another AI provider or model.* For OpenRouter, which chooses the upstream
  inference provider for each request, it promises the service and the model:
  *…use {model} through OpenRouter; the study does not switch them to another
  AI service or model. OpenRouter may use a different ZDR-compatible upstream
  provider for each request.* An interview saved under this setting can be
  re-analyzed only with that provider and model. After a switch, a retry is
  refused (`PROVIDER_NOT_DISCLOSED`) until the study is set back.
- **The provider or model may change.** The notice adds: *The researcher may
  later analyze your responses with a different AI provider or model.*

Studies saved before this release keep their notice, and their interviews are
not checked, until the study is saved again. Studies approved by an ethics
board may need an amendment before switching.
([#69](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/pull/69))

## Limits

- **Researcher AI budget** on both standalone targets. Every
  researcher-initiated provider call is charged first: preview greeting, turn
  and synthesis, aggregate, follow-up and analysis retry. Limits apply per
  session and per workspace (README, Researcher AI request limits). A retry that is refused, or a
  replay of an earlier one, is free. Hosted mode keeps its own limiter.
  ([#71](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/pull/71))
- **Sign-in.** On Cloudflare, IPv6 clients now share one budget per /64, so a
  host rotating addresses can no longer use up the global window (owner
  amendment to RT-07). **Node standalone now limits sign-in attempts too**:
  10 failures per client per 15 minutes and 200 an hour, the same as
  Cloudflare. ([#72](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/pull/72))

## Cloudflare

- The sample workspace's aggregate and follow-up now work on the AI Gateway
  transport. The seeded interviews are synthetic and need no disclosure;
  every other interview is still checked.
  ([#68](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/pull/68))
- `update --forget-provider-key <p[,p]>` records provider keys removed by hand
  with `wrangler secret delete`.
  ([#70](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/pull/70))
- `verify` no longer warns about the gateway fields `internal` and
  `wholesale`. Both are undocumented. `wholesale` needs no check (`byok_only`
  is required and every request carries `cf-aig-no-wholesale: true`), and
  `internal` warns if it is ever `true`.
  ([#67](https://github.com/linxule/openinterviewer/pull/67))

## Dependencies

- jose 6 (session tokens: HS256 as before; existing sessions stay valid).
- Dependabot now holds tailwind-merge and ESLint majors until Tailwind 4 and
  ESLint 10 plugin support land.

## Upgrade notes

- **Node standalone:** sign-in now needs `RATE_LIMIT_SALT` (32+ characters)
  and the Redis variables, all of which readiness and `setup:check` already
  required. The sign-in body is capped at 1 KiB, so an `ADMIN_PASSWORD`
  longer than about 1,000 characters is reported as `admin_password_too_long`.
  Malformed sign-in JSON now answers 400, not 500. Behind a host that does not
  overwrite the forwarding headers, a client can choose its own sign-in key
  (README).
- **Rolling back to 4.1.x:** studies saved with the new provider setting are
  refused by the older release's validator. They fail closed, and no data
  reaches another provider. Everything else rolls back as a compatible redeploy.
- No schema migration and no configuration change on Cloudflare. Update with
  `npm run setup:cloudflare -- update`.
