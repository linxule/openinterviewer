Verbatim

Typography

Two laws, lint-enforced through primitives. Everything else is convention.

Law 1 · Source Serif 4 — verbatim human speech, consent text, interpretation prose “The headline made me curious, but I was not ready to give it twenty minutes.” If a serif appears anywhere that is not someone's words or a reading document, it is a bug.
Public Sans — all interface chrome Buttons, labels, navigation, interviewer questions, forms. A face designed for public digital services, for a product whose ethic is records, consent, and ownership. Not Inter.
IBM Plex Mono — machine-verifiable facts turn 12 · exploration · synthesized by claude-opus · study rev 3 · receipt a91f3c · tabular-nums
Scale Display 56/58 serif 400 · bottom line 28/40 · page title 24/32 sans 600 · verbatim body 19/31 serif · UI 15/24 · meta 13/20 · coordinate 12/16 mono · labels 11px at 0.08em (never 0.18em).

The signature moment

Click a claim's citation mark and the quoted turn unfolds beside it, baseline-aligned, with its coordinate. Reading position never jumps; checking provenance costs a glance — a promise that is cheap to check is a promise people actually check. The journeys

Participant — the flagship

Two registers, one transcript model (A1). The live interview carries no visible apparatus — no turn numbers, no citation marks. One column: interviewer questions in muted sans, answers in full-ink serif, warm paper, phase as a humane sentence ("Part two of four · exploring what you said"), never dots.

The input becomes an auto-growing textarea set in the same serif the answer will be read in — the current single-line input that submits on Enter punishes exactly the behavior qualitative research exists to collect. Desktop: Enter is a newline, Cmd/Ctrl+Enter sends. Mobile: only the Send button sends. Placeholder: "Take as much space as you need." No character counter.

Consent is a signed document — serif at reading measure, provider disclosure as a mono fact block, the server's acceptedAt shown back. The interview ends in a receipt: turns contributed, elapsed time, consent timestamp, and Download your transcript — the words belong to the speaker.

Failure states: filled terracotta block, existing fail-closed copy verbatim. Never a plausible placeholder, never a skeleton.

Researcher — the return surface

A real app shell at last: persistent rail with Studies · Settings · account, current-study breadcrumb, a quiet mono footer — your keys · your database. Dashboards are ruled register tables — scannable, keyboard-navigable, dense; the reading measure applies to documents, never to tables (A2).

Synthesis is the signature surface: bottom line as one serif sentence, themed passages with wine citation marks, the margin-unfold trace, and a plain-language provenance footer — synthesized by <model> · study rev N · timestamp · receipt hash — data that exists today and is never shown. No user ever sees the words "colophon," "apparatus," or "marginalia" (A2).

Study setup becomes a revisable document — sections readable at measure with per-section inline editing, making studyRevision legible — instead of a 1,599-line mega-form.

Demo & landing

The demo keeps its state machine and accessibility engineering wholesale — the a11y suite is a review gate on the redesign PR — and becomes the faithful miniature of the real trace. The landing leads with a specimen, not a description: a real quote, its margin note, its coordinate, above the fold, before any copy. The tagline becomes the caption to the demonstration.

Three initiatives

Amendment A5: this is three initiatives wearing one coat — so they get separate PR trains and separate gates. The visual revamp is never held hostage to schema migration.

Initiative 1 · shippable alone

Design revamp

Tokens → fonts via next/font (none exists today) → cn() and ~10 primitives (Page, Margin, Rule, Label, Coordinate, Turn, Citation, Disclosure, Button ×3, Field) → participant typography + textarea → app shell + register dashboard → demo reskin → study setup → landing. Mergeable with evidence still unlinked.

Gate · A9 — before the participant flagship completes: a five-participant moderated test probing for self-editing ("did you consider how your answer would look to the researcher?"), with the fallback agreed in writing — if transcript register chills disclosure, the participant view drops to a conversational layout and the document register stays researcher-side. Plus a researcher walkthrough scored on time-to-find-a-quote.
Initiative 2 · fast-follow

Evidence traceability

EvidenceRef { quote, turnIndex, interviewId } replaces the free-text evidence string; synthesis prompt updated; validation by normalized matching — Unicode quote/dash canonicalization, whitespace collapse, ellipsis-aware containment — never naive substring, with a measured false-rejection budget, a researcher-visible repair path, and rejection telemetry from day one. Then the trace ships on real data.

Why it exists — the provenance thesis is not in the data model today; the demo can only trace because its evidence is hardcoded. A fabricated quote rejects the synthesis: fail-closed, extended to interpretation itself.
Initiative 3 · own reviews

Features

Participant transcript download (through the privacy/consent review the README's boundaries require) · the merged transcript rail in interview detail and the aggregate concordance — both required to reuse the canonical trace primitive (A7) · typeset HTML/PDF export.

The nine amendments

From the adversarial challenge (verdict: proceed amended). Per A3, three-model convergence is struck as evidence everywhere in this document — the direction stands on repo facts and the gates above, and every decision remains re-openable.

A1 — Two registers, one transcript model
Participant live view renders transcript grammar without visible apparatus; coordinates and citation marks are researcher-side and receipt-side. Fallback to a conversational layout pre-agreed in writing if testing shows chilled disclosure. "Dignified" and "observed" are the same aesthetic read from different power positions.
A2 — Mechanics stay, genre vocabulary goes
No user-facing "colophon / apparatus / marginalia." Provenance shown as plain facts. Triage density is a requirement: scannable rows, keyboard nav, no reading measure on tables. The trace is Dovetail's marketed differentiator wearing literary clothes — the mechanics are industry-standard value.
A3 — Void the unanimity argument
"Three independent models converged" is not evidence — the brief steered the genre. The direction stands on the gap between the product's thesis and its UI, which is a repo fact no brief manufactured.
A4 — Enforce the law in components and lint
Semantics scoped inside primitives; no free-floating serif or wine/ochre utilities; five-line ESLint guard in CI; the law cut to two rules. A convention rots by month three; a linted primitive does not.
A5 — Partition into three initiatives
Design revamp ships without the trace. EvidenceRef uses normalized matching with a false-rejection budget and repair UX — naive substring validation would reject faithful syntheses in a product where failure is loud and no fallback is allowed.
A6 — Participant flow is light-only
No OS auto-scheme lottery on the most trust-critical screen; one tested palette for a one-shot audience. Dark is a researcher toggle. Earn a participant dark mode later with evidence, not romance.
A7 — One canonical trace
The margin-unfold is built once as a primitive, gated on the a11y suite and 375 px. The interview-detail rail and the aggregate concordance must reuse it — if they can't, the shared-vocabulary claim was false and the surfaces get rethought, not forked.
A8 — Disclosure keeps interruptive weight
Filled band, not hairline. Beauty and salience compete on disclosure surfaces; salience wins. Arm's-length-on-a-phone prominence check for the synthetic label.
A9 — Human validation gate
Five participants, four researchers, one week — after tokens and primitives, before the flagship commits. If the direction is right the gates cost a week; if it is wrong about participants they save the project.
